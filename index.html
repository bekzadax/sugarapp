FAQ
Is there a token required?
No, the basic matching functionality is free. Premium features like "Super Likes" or "Global Search" may require holding our governance token or specific NFTs in the future.
What chains do you support?
Currently, SUGAR is native to Solana due to its low transaction fees and high speed, which is essential for a social experience. Ethereum L2 support is on our Q4 roadmap.
Can I stay anonymous?
Yes. You can choose to display your ENS/Bonfida name or remain completely pseudonymous. We verify assets, not identities.

1. Introduction

Welcome to SUGAR. SUGAR is a Web3 social matching platform that connects crypto users based on their on-chain portfolio activity. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the application at sugar-app.xyz.

2. Information We Collect

  • Wallet Address: When you connect a Solana wallet, we read your public wallet address. We never request or store your private keys or seed phrases.
  • On-chain Portfolio Data: We fetch publicly available token holdings and portfolio balances from the Solana blockchain to power matching and the KOL Leaderboard.
  • Profile Information: Display name, profile photo (optional), gender preference, and bio that you voluntarily provide.
  • Activity Data: Hearts sent/received, posts, comments, votes, and messages you create within the app.
  • Device & Usage Data: Browser type, operating system, and interaction logs for analytics and debugging.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To match you with compatible crypto users based on portfolio overlap.
  • To display your profile and activity to other users on the platform.
  • To deliver in-app notifications and private messages.
  • To maintain and improve the SUGAR platform.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse.

4. Information Sharing

SUGAR does not sell personal information. Data may be shared:

  • With other users, as intended (e.g., your public profile and posts).
  • With service providers (e.g., Supabase for database hosting) under confidentiality agreements.
  • If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of SUGAR or its users.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Supabase and in your browser's local storage. Reasonable security measures are implemented, but no system is 100% secure. Blockchain data (wallet addresses and transactions) is inherently public.

6. Your Rights

  • Disconnect your wallet at any time to stop new data collection.
  • Request deletion of your profile and associated data by contacting us.
  • Update your profile information within the app at any time.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

SUGAR uses browser local storage to persist your session, profile, and activity across visits. No third-party advertising cookies are used.

8. Children's Privacy

SUGAR is intended for users 18 years of age or older. Data from minors is not knowingly collected. If you believe a minor has provided data, please contact support immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The updated version will be posted with a revised effective date. Continued use of SUGAR after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, reach us at support@sugar-app.xyz.
